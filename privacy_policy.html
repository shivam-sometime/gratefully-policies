Privacy Policy for Gratefully

Last Updated: October 2025

Gratefully – Gratitude Journal
Developed by Satvik InfoByte

1. Information We Collect & Third-Party Tools

Your Journaling Data Stays Private: Gratefully is built with privacy as a foundational principle. We do not collect, read, store, or sell any of your personal journal entries, reflections, prompts, mood logs, or attached photos. All your journal content is stored locally on your device and remains solely under your control.

Analytics & Performance: We use Google Firebase (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics) to collect aggregate, anonymized diagnostics, performance metrics, and crash logs. This helps us troubleshoot crashes, improve stability, and refine app usability.

Attribution & Marketing Measurement: We integrate the Meta Platforms, Inc. (Facebook SDK) to measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns and app installation attribution. The Meta SDK may collect standard technical device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / AAID), device type, operating system version, and general app events (such as app install or subscription purchases).

Strict Privacy Commitment: Your private journal content, written thoughts, and reflections are never shared with Meta, Google, or any advertising network, and are never used for targeted advertising or user profiling.

2. Google Drive Backup

Gratefully offers an optional Google Drive backup feature to help users save and restore their journal entries. When you enable this feature, the app requests your permission to access your Google Drive account. Your data is only stored in your personal Google Drive and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

You are in full control of your backups and can delete them from your Google Drive at any time. We do not have access to your Google Drive files.

3. In-App Purchases

Some features, such as Google Drive backup, may require a one-time purchase or subscription through Google Play’s in-app billing system. We do not collect or store any payment information. All transactions are securely handled by Google Play, in accordance with their policies.

4. Internet Access

While most features of Gratefully work offline, the Google Drive backup and in-app purchase features require an internet connection. Other than these optional features, the app does not communicate with external servers.

5. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Diagnostic and measurement data is processed by our service providers in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase: Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook): Meta Privacy Policy

Your Advertising Choices: You can manage, reset, or opt out of personalized ad tracking and Android Advertising ID (AAID) collection at any time through your device settings (e.g., Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

6. Security

Since all primary data is stored on your device, we recommend using your device's security features (e.g., PIN, fingerprint) to keep your entries safe. For Google Drive backups, security is managed by your Google account and its associated protections.

7. Policy Changes

This privacy policy may be updated if the app’s features change in the future. Users will be informed of such updates via the app.
